# Gift photo prompts (ChatGPT image generation)

All gift photos share one clean style, so the collection looks like a single set.

**How to use:** open ChatGPT, attach the gift's current photo from this folder (or save it from the shop link) as a reference, and paste that gift's prompt. Save the result as the file name shown, e.g. `iittala-aino-pair.png`, and upload it to this folder, then delete the old `.jpg` with the same name (or just send the pictures to Claude to swap in). The new picture shows the next time the site updates.

**Check before uploading:** the colours, pattern, number of pieces and shape must match the real product. Guests receive the real item.

## Shared style (already included in every prompt below)

> Square 1:1 product photo, 1600 × 1600 px. Pure white seamless background (#FFFFFF). The product is centred and fills about 70% of the frame, seen from slightly above at a gentle three-quarter angle. Soft, even studio light from the upper left, with a faint natural contact shadow under the product only. Crisp focus, true-to-life colours, premium catalogue look. No packaging, no gift box, no props, no hands, no text, no watermark, no added logos beyond those printed on the product itself.
